Product Innovation

STEM Pionir: Robotics for the Next Generation

Designing and manufacturing Bosnia’s first screen-free STEM toy. This was a platform built from the ground up, requiring a fusion of mechanical engineering, electronic design, and pedagogical strategy.

⚙️ The Technical Challenge

Situation
Educational robotics kits were either plastic-heavy toys with low durability or complex systems requiring screens and immediate coding knowledge, creating high barriers to entry.
Task
Develop a modular, durable, and screen-free robotics kit that teaches fundamental mechanics and electronics to children aged 7-15.
Action
Engineered a modular wooden construction system using real mechanical fasteners. Designed basic electronic circuits with DC motors to teach physics principles without software complexity.
Result
Successfully launched a sustainable learning platform used in 15+ schools, improving spatial intelligence and mechanical debugging skills in thousands of children.

🎓 The "Invention Engine" Philosophy

The Problem: Disposable Learning

Most STEM toys follow a "build once" manual. Once the model is complete, the learning stops, and the toy is abandoned, offering no long-term engagement or creative freedom.

The Solution: Open-Ended Tinkering

Designed the STEM Pionir as a constructionist toolset. The same components build drawing robots, catapults, or vehicles. We shifted focus from "What to build" to "How mechanics work," fostering a growth mindset.
